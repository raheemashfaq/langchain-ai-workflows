
Dog Nutrition

Dogs need a balanced diet containing appropriate nutrients.
Dogs with sensitive stomachs may benefit from a consistent diet.
Introduce dietary changes gradually.
Consult a veterinarian if digestive problems persist.

Dog Hydration

Dogs need access to fresh, clean drinking water throughout the day.
Monitor changes in drinking habits.

Dog Grooming

Regular grooming helps keep a dog's coat clean.
Brush according to the dog's coat type.
Inspect the skin for irritation.
